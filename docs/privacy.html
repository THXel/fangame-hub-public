THXel Twitch Extensions

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the THXel Fangame Recap and PinguLexa Community Mission Twitch Extensions, as well as the public THXel Fangame Hub pages used by those Extensions.

2. Operator and contact

The Extensions are operated by THXel in Germany. For privacy questions or requests, use the public contact links provided on THXel's Twitch channel.

3. Data handled by the Extensions

The Extensions do not ask viewers to share their Twitch identity and do not collect or store viewer names, Twitch user IDs, email addresses, chat messages, payment information, Bits activity, subscriptions or Channel Points activity.

No advertising, analytics, tracking pixels or operator-controlled cookies are used. The Extensions do not create browser-local profiles. Broadcaster settings are stored using Twitch's Extension configuration service and contain only technical display settings.

4. Public content requests

To show fangame information and community-mission progress, the Extensions request public JSON files and images hosted on GitHub Pages. These requests may disclose standard technical connection data, including an IP address, request time, requested file, browser information and referring page, to GitHub as the hosting provider. THXel does not receive or control GitHub's infrastructure logs.

GitHub explains its processing in the GitHub Privacy Statement and its GitHub Pages data-collection notice.

5. Twitch

Twitch hosts and displays the Extensions. Twitch may process technical, account and usage data independently under its own privacy terms. This policy covers only processing controlled by THXel and does not replace Twitch's privacy notices.

6. External links

The Fangame Recap may link to the full Fangame Hub and to game pages on Delicious Fruit. The Community Mission may link to the full Fangame Hub. External pages open only after a viewer chooses to leave Twitch. The privacy terms of the destination provider apply after the link is opened.

7. Retention and sharing

THXel does not maintain a viewer database for these Extensions and therefore does not retain or sell viewer personal data. Technical data processed by Twitch, GitHub or an external destination is retained and shared according to that provider's own policies and legal obligations.

8. Your rights

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, and you may object to processing. You may also lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection supervisory authority. Contact THXel using the channel link above for questions about operator-controlled processing. Requests concerning Twitch, GitHub or another external provider should be directed to that provider.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated if the Extensions, hosting setup or legal requirements change. The current version and update date will remain available on this page.
